About the Mission

Called to serve. Created to share His Word.

Praise Jesus Divine Word Mission is a Christ-centered ministry focused on Bible research, Bible theology, Bible science, Scripture-based teaching, Gospel ministry, evangelism and making disciples. We seek to study God's Word carefully, teach biblical truth and help people grow in faith through Christian education and discipleship.

We are a family of believers from every walk of life, united by faith and committed to growing together in the knowledge of God's Word. Whether you are exploring faith for the first time or have walked with Christ for years, there is a place for you here.
